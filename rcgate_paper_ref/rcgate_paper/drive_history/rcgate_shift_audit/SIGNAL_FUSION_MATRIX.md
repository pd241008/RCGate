## Reading of the matrix

**Demonstrated.** Flat or linear fusion of related scores is a standard baseline and often wins or ties a more elaborate mixer (local A4 over A7; Bakman's linear model over a tree). A designed conditional formula can still beat a tree when the formula matches the data-generating split, which is what FRANQ reports against XGBoost on AlignScore plus two uncertainty scores.

**Not demonstrated.** A metadata-free gate over external cross-model and evidence families, evaluated on RAGTruth with sources, tasks, and generators held out, beating a flat model that sees the same numbers.

**Inference.** Adding dispersion, NLI margin, or family-disagreement features does not create a new algorithm. It creates features. The fair test is whether those features help a flat model under the Dubanowska constraints. A gate is justified only if it beats that flat model after the features are available to both.
